Metallica quotes 

## Nothing else matters quotes
The phrase "Nothing Else Matters" is most famously associated with Metallica's iconic 1991 power ballad, written by frontman James Hetfield about trust, intimacy, and staying true to oneself while on the road. Beyond the song, variations of the theme appear across literature and philosophy about what truly counts in life.
Metallica — Nothing Else Matters (Key Lyrics)

"So close, no matter how far / Couldn't be much more from the heart / Forever trusting who we are / And nothing else matters." 
"Never opened myself this way / Life is ours, we live it our way / All these words, I don't just say / And nothing else matters." 
"Trust I seek and I find in you / Every day for us something new / Open mind for a different view / And nothing else matters." 
"Never cared for what they do / Never cared for what they know / But I know." 

## On What Truly Matters in Life
"In the end, only three things matter: how much you loved, how gently you lived, and how gracefully you let go of things not meant for you." — Attributed to Buddha / Jack Kornfield
"When you love someone, you love the person as they are, and not as you'd like them to be." — Leo Tolstoy
"At the end of the day, if you can say you did your best, made someone smile, and stayed true to yourself, nothing else matters."
"Focus on what matters and let go of what doesn't."
