## Bob Dylan philosophical quotes

Bob Dylan, recipient of the 2016 Nobel Prize in Literature, redefined songwriting by fusing folk traditions with poetic, existential, and social philosophy. Across his lyrics, interviews, and memoirs, his words capture the impermanence of life, the nature of truth, and individual freedom.

##On Time, Change, and Impermanence
"He who is not busy being born is busy dying." — It's Alright, Ma (I'm Only Bleeding)
"There is nothing so stable as change."
"Behind me do not walk for I may not lead and ahead of me do not walk for I may not follow. Walk beside me so that we may be one."
"Ah, but I was so much older then, I'm younger than that now." — My Back Pages


##On Success, Identity, and Freedom
"A man is a success if he gets up in the morning and gets to bed at night and in between does what he wants to do."
"To live outside the law, you must be honest." — Absolutely Sweet Marie
"You can't be wise and in love at the same time."
"Destiny is a feeling you have that you know something's gonna happen and it's not even happened yet." — 60 Minutes Interview (2004)


##On Truth, Society, and Morality
"When you got nothing, you got nothing to lose." — Like a Rolling Stone
"Don't criticize what you can't understand." — The Times They Are A-Changin'
"Money doesn't talk, it swears." — It's Alright, Ma (I'm Only Bleeding)
"All the truth in the world adds up to one big lie." — Things Have Changed


##On Art, Inspiration, and Purpose
"A hero is somebody who understands the responsibility that comes with his freedom."
"Creativity has much to do with experience, observation, and imagination, and if any one of those key elements is missing, it doesn't work." — Chronicles: Volume One
"Take care of all your memories. For you cannot relive them." — Open the Door, Homer
