Solzenetzin famous quotes

–Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (Nobel Prize in Literature in 1970)

Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (1918–2008), the Nobel Prize-winning Russian author and Soviet dissident, is best known for uncovering the horrors of the Soviet labor camp system in works such as The Gulag Archipelago and One Day in the Life of Ivan Denisovich. 



# On Good, Evil, and Human Nature
"The line dividing good and evil cuts through the heart of every human being. And who is willing to destroy a piece of his own heart?" — The Gulag Archipelago 

"Gradually it was disclosed to me that the line separating good and evil passes not through states, nor between classes, nor between political parties—but right through every human heart." — The Gulag Archipelago 
–Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (Nobel Prize for Literature 1970)

"To do evil a human being must first of all believe that what he's doing is good... Ideology—that is what gives evildooing its long-sought justification." — The Gulag Archipelago 
Academy of Ideas


# On Lies, Truth, and Freedom
"We know that they are lying, they know that they are lying, they even know that we know they are lying... but they are still lying." 

"One drop of truth can outweigh an ocean of lies." 

"In our country, the lie has become not just a moral category, but a pillar of the State." — 1974 Interview 

"The simple step of a courageous individual is not to take part in the lie." — Live Not By Lies


#On Power, Tyranny, and Resilience
"You only have power over people so long as you don't take everything away from them. But when you've robbed a man of everything, he's no longer in your power. He's free again." — The First Circle / Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (Nobel Prize for Literature 1970)

"For a country to have a great writer is like having another government. That's why no regime has ever loved great writers, only minor ones." — The First Circle 

"How can you expect a man who's warm to understand one who's cold?" — One Day in the Life of Ivan Denisovich 

# On Living Wisely
"Own only what you can always carry with you: know languages, know countries, know people. Let your memory be your travel bag." — The Gulag Archipelago 
–Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (Nobel Prize for Literature 1970)

"Human beings are born with different capacities. If they are free, they are not equal. And if they are equal, they are not free." 
–Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (Nobel Prize for Literature 1970)
